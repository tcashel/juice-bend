Contract and limits
  session-usage SNAPSHOT reads one immutable accepted capture from physical record 0.
  The reader owns its deferred read transaction; refusal, failure, limit and
  cancellation paths preserve any transaction it did not start.
  Defaults: 1,000,000 physical records; 1,073,741,824 verified bytes;
  100,000 exact Claude call keys; 16,777,216 logical UTF8 descriptor bytes.
  Configurable descriptor maximum: 67,108,864 bytes. At most 16 raw witnesses.
  Raw payload windows: at most 128 map candidates/about 1 MiB accounted bytes,
  with a first-row progress exception. Header/map guards can scan the whole map.
  JSON plus newline is bounded to 1 MiB; output is rejected rather than truncated.
  Scan completion, required-bucket completeness and attribution completeness
  are separate. Unknown buckets remain null/unknown, never invented zero.
  Claude uses exact call keys and per-bucket maxima across windows. Codex keeps
  ordinary/fork/reviewer/unknown categories separate; version-dependent fork
  fallback is disclosed and cannot claim complete attribution. No billing,
  quota, prices, accounts, network or cross-library usage total is asserted.
